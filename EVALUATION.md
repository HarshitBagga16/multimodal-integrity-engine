## Edge cases and limitations

1. **Silent video:** `13_silent_video.mp4` has no extracted speech. The pipeline reports it as empty without crashing. It cannot compare content that was not extracted.

2. **Unequal document lengths:** the renewable-energy video transcript is shorter than the PDF and DOCX. Coverage can differ by direction; a 100% score in one direction does not mean the entire pair is identical.

3. **Chunk identification:** the network-security DOCX copies show only 50% coverage in the current matrix despite being exact copies. This indicates a likely chunk accounting/scoring limitation that should be fixed or disclosed.

4. **Common boilerplate:** generic phrases shared by unrelated documents could trigger matches. A production system should down-weight common boilerplate and require meaningful matching text.

5. **AI-writing estimate:** writing style alone cannot reliably prove that a document was generated by AI. This report does not claim to validate AI authorship.

## Interpretation and trade-offs

These results measure performance on a small, deliberately constructed synthetic dataset. They are not evidence of equivalent accuracy on real submissions. The current non-zero-coverage rule is intentionally simple and may over-flag weak matches. A stronger evaluation would tune a minimum semantic-similarity threshold on a larger labelled dataset, evaluate exact copies separately from paraphrases and cross-modal matches, and report precision/recall for each category.

The silent video is excluded from pairwise metrics because no text was extracted; its graceful handling is assessed as an edge-case test.
